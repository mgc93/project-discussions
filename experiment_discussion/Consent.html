Study Overview

This study examines how facilitators affect the quality of online discussions on topics of public interest. It consists of three parts:

  • Part 1 – AI Interview (~20 minutes): A one-on-one conversation with an AI on a topic of public interest (e.g., housing policy, education reform, or technology regulation). The AI will ask open-ended questions to help you explore and articulate your views. You can respond by voice or by typing. Afterwards, you will be paired with another participant for Part 2; this may take up to 10 minutes.
  • Part 2 – Live Conversation (~20 minutes): A text-based discussion with another participant on the same topic, supported by two additional participants:
    • Facilitator: May introduce prompts, ask questions, offer summaries, or suggest different perspectives without arguing for any particular position.
    • AI Assistant: Helps ensure balanced participation and that each participant gets to express their views.
  • Part 3 – Survey (~20 minutes): A short survey about your experience. You may leave at any time.

Time Commitment: Approximately 60 minutes total.

Compensation: You will receive the agreed payment through the recruitment platform. The researchers only collect anonymous data and do not have access to your payment information.

There are no right or wrong answers. We are interested in how people experience facilitated conversations and what makes them productive.

Eligibility

You are eligible to take part in this study if you:

  • Are at least 18 years old
  • Are fluent in English
  • Have normal or corrected-to-normal vision

Voluntary Participation and Withdrawal

Your participation is voluntary. You can discontinue the study at any time without providing a reason. During the AI interview, you may stop at any time. During the live conversation, you may leave at any time by clicking the "Leave Conversation" button. If you withdraw before completing the study, you will receive partial compensation for the time you spent, but your data will not be used and will be deleted prior to analysis.

After you complete the study and receive the full debriefing, you will have the option to withdraw your data within 7 days if you wish. Instructions for data withdrawal will be provided in the debriefing.

Data Privacy and Usage

No information will be collected that could be used to identify you to the researchers (such as your name, email address, or detailed location). Your data will be identified only by a randomly generated ID number.

We will record the full text of your AI interview and your live conversation for research purposes. Your conversation data will be collected through the DeliberateLab research platform, which is hosted on Google Cloud infrastructure. The data will then be exported and stored on secure, encrypted servers at the Complexity Science Hub. Conversation transcripts will be stored separately from any identifying information. No personally identifiable information is collected through the platform. Your data will be identified only by an anonymous ID.

Your anonymised data may be retained for scientific reuse, in line with research data management standards and GDPR requirements. If anonymised transcripts are shared publicly, any potentially identifying information will be redacted. The aggregate results of the study may be published in scientific journals and conference presentations.

Please note: During the live conversation, the other participant will see what you write. Please do not share personally identifying information (such as your name, address, or workplace) in the conversation.

Risks and Benefits

Risks: The risks associated with this study are minimal. During the AI interview, you may be asked to reflect on and articulate your views on a potentially controversial topic, which may cause mild discomfort. During the live conversation, you will discuss this topic with another participant who may hold different views, which may also cause mild discomfort. The Facilitator and AI Assistant are designed to support respectful conversation and will intervene if needed. You may leave either part at any time.

Benefits: There are no direct benefits to you from participating in this research beyond monetary compensation. However, your participation will contribute to scientific understanding of online conversations and facilitated discussion.
