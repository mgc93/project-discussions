Part 1: AI Interview

Before we begin, please read the following instructions carefully.

In this first part of this study, you will have a one-on-one conversation with an AI interviewer about a specific topic.

The AI will ask you a small number of open-ended questions. There are no right or wrong answers. We are simply interested in your personal views and how you think about the topic.

This part will take approximately 10–15 minutes.
